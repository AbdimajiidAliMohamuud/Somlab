Who we are

A laboratory solutions company with a long-term view.

Founded in 2022 as a sister company of Medisom Health Group, Somlab Diagnostics imports, distributes and installs quality laboratory equipment, reagents and supplies.

We support customers beyond delivery through training, maintenance, laboratory management systems and customised turnkey projects.
